    Feature flags

    Paid plans Pro & Max

    One switch covers both paid tiers. Off: both cards show “Coming Soon” with a Notify-me button. On: Pro (₹299 / ₹2,249) and Max (₹499 / ₹3,749) become purchasable. The Google Play service-account creds must already be set in Vercel or checkout fails.

    —
    GIF keyboard

    Global kill-switch for the GIF tab in the emoji panel. Off: /api/gif stops serving and the tab shows “GIFs unavailable”. Needs a Giphy key in Vercel to work at all.

    —
    Referrals

    Invite codes and the bonus tokens they grant. Off by default: the app hides the invite row entirely, so turning this on is what makes the feature exist.

    —
    Bring Your Own Key legacy

    BYOK was removed from the app. This only reaches old builds still in the wild (≤ v2.0.x). Off: those users fall back to the Free quota. Stored keys are kept.

    —

    Free tier limits

    These change what existing users get, on every device at once.
