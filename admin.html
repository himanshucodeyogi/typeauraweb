Remote config

Feature switches applied on each device's next ping — no app release needed.

Paid plans Pro & Max

One switch covers both paid tiers. When off, both cards show “Coming Soon” with a Notify-me button. When on, Pro (₹299 / ₹2,249) and Max (₹499 / ₹3,749) become purchasable — the Google Play service-account creds must already be set in Vercel or checkout fails.

—
GIF keyboard

Global kill-switch for the GIF tab in the emoji panel. When off, /api/gif stops serving and the tab shows “GIFs unavailable”. Needs a Giphy key in Vercel to work at all.

—
Referrals

Invite codes and the bonus tokens they grant. Off by default — the app hides the invite row entirely, so turning this on is what makes the feature exist.

—
Free daily tokens pricing

The Free tier's daily budget, applied to every device at once. Check scripts/token-distribution.js before moving it — the number to read is the wall cadence for engaged users, not the average.

Bring Your Own Key legacy

BYOK was removed from the app — this only reaches old builds still in the wild (≤ v2.0.x). When off, those users fall back to the Free quota. Stored keys are kept.

—
Gated AI tools

Tools selected here are locked behind a paid plan for every device. Leave all off to keep the whole AI suite available on the Free tier.
