FAQ

Questions, answered

Privacy, permissions, pricing and limits: everything people ask before installing.

Still have a question? Email us

Is TypeAura really free?

Yes. Every AI feature (the Floating Lens, Live Translate, voice typing, AI Chat and all six AI tools) runs on the free plan, on a daily allowance that resets every midnight. No card, no login, no trial period. Pro and Max are optional subscriptions for people who need more headroom.

Do you store what I type?

No. Your keystrokes, passwords and OTPs never leave your device. Text is sent to our server only when you explicitly tap an AI action, such as translate, rewrite or reply, and it is processed and returned immediately, never stored. Read the full detail in our Privacy Policy.

Why does the Floating Lens need Accessibility permission?

The Lens translates text that belongs to other apps, so it needs Android's Accessibility API to read the text you tap on. It reads only at the moment you tap, and it explicitly skips password fields and editable text boxes. The permission is entirely optional: the keyboard and every AI tool work without it.

Can I cancel Pro or Max?

Yes, any time. Pro and Max are auto-renewing Google Play subscriptions, billed monthly or yearly. Cancel in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions, or from the Manage subscription link inside the app. Cancelling stops the next charge. You keep everything you paid for until the end of the period you are already in, then drop back to the Free budget.

How do I buy Pro or Max?

Inside the app, not on this website. Install TypeAura, open Settings → Plans, pick Pro or Max, and pay through Google Play. You will get a TA-XXXX-XXXX recovery code. Save it: that code is how you move your plan to a new device or restore it after a reinstall.

Which languages are supported?

129, and the keyboard picks the right ones for you. It detects the country it is installed in and leads every language list with what people there actually speak, with 176 countries tuned individually.

Alongside the languages it carries 21 romanizations, the way people write a language in Latin letters when its own script will not fit on a phone keyboard: Hinglish in India, Arabizi across the Arab world, Banglish, Roman Urdu, Greeklish, Roman Persian and fifteen more.

Hinglish is still what TypeAura is really built for: type the way you actually speak and it converts to clean English, or the reverse.

Does it work offline?

Typing, glide typing, suggestions, themes, emoji and clipboard history all work fully offline. The AI features (translation, the Lens, AI Chat and the tools) need an internet connection, because the model runs on a server rather than on your phone.

What happens when I run out of AI for the day?

The keyboard keeps working normally. Only the AI actions pause, and your allowance resets automatically at midnight. If you hit the limit regularly, Pro gives you much more every day, and Max gives you three times what Pro does.

Is the keyboard layout right for my language?

There are seven layouts: QWERTY, AZERTY, QWERTZ, and dedicated variants for Spanish (Ñ), German (Ü Ö Ä), Danish/Norwegian (Å Æ Ø) and Swedish/Finnish (Å Ö Ä). Those letters get real keys rather than long-presses, because they are letters of the alphabet, not accents.

Your country picks the starting layout and you can change it any time in Settings. Accented letters beyond the layout are a long-press away, and the currency key types the sign your country actually spends in.

Which Android versions work? Is there an iPhone version?

TypeAura runs on Android 5.0 and above. There is no iOS version, because iOS does not allow the kind of system-wide overlay the Floating Lens depends on. There is a Windows desktop app with the same AI tools and a global-hotkey Lens.
