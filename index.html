Worldwide

Built for how your country types

TypeAura started in India, with Hinglish. It turns out the rest of the world has the same problem — so the keyboard now adapts to wherever it is installed.

Country-aware

It already knows what you speak

On first launch TypeAura works out which country it is in and leads every language list — onboarding, the Lens, Live Translate, the mic — with the languages people there actually speak, most common first. No hunting through an alphabetical list of 129 to find your own.

The layout, the currency key and even the spelling follow. A French install starts on AZERTY, a German one gets real Ü Ö Ä keys, the currency key types ₹ in India and ₦ in Nigeria, and "Fix Grammar" stops turning colour into color outside America.

Floating Lens

Translate anything on your screen

A floating bubble that lives over every app. Tap any text — a message, a caption, a notification, a button — and TypeAura instantly reads and translates it. No copy-paste, no switching apps.

How it works

Tap. Read. Translate.

Turn on the Floating Lens and a small bubble floats above whatever you're using. Tap the text you want to understand and the Lens reads it on the spot, then shows the translation in a clean pop-up — right where you're looking.

It works in WhatsApp, Instagram, browsers, games — literally any app. And it's privacy-safe: the Lens automatically skips password and editable fields, and never stores what it reads.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything people ask before installing — privacy, permissions, pricing and limits.

Is TypeAura really free?

Yes. Every AI feature — the Floating Lens, Live Translate, voice typing, AI Chat and all six AI tools — runs on a free budget of 10,000 tokens per day that resets every midnight. No card, no login, no trial period. Pro and Max are optional subscriptions for people who need more headroom.

Do you store what I type?

No. Your keystrokes, passwords and OTPs never leave your device. Text is sent to our server only when you explicitly tap an AI action — translate, rewrite, reply — and it is processed and returned immediately, never stored. Read the full detail in our Privacy Policy.

Why does the Floating Lens need Accessibility permission?

The Lens translates text that belongs to other apps, so it needs Android's Accessibility API to read the text you tap on. It reads only at the moment you tap, and it explicitly skips password fields and editable text boxes. The permission is entirely optional — the keyboard and every AI tool work without it.

Can I cancel Pro or Max?

Yes, any time. Pro and Max are auto-renewing Google Play subscriptions, billed monthly or yearly. Cancel in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions, or from the Manage subscription link inside the app. Cancelling stops the next charge — you keep everything you paid for until the end of the period you are already in, then drop back to the Free budget.

How do I buy Pro or Max?

Inside the app, not on this website. Install TypeAura, open Settings → Plans, pick Pro or Max, and pay through Google Play. You will get a TA-XXXX-XXXX recovery code — save it, as that code is how you move your plan to a new device or restore it after a reinstall.

Which languages are supported?

129 — and the keyboard picks the right ones for you. It detects the country it is installed in and leads every language list with what people there actually speak, with 176 countries tuned individually.

Alongside the languages it carries 21 romanizations — the way people write a language in Latin letters when its own script will not fit on a phone keyboard. Hinglish in India, Arabizi across the Arab world, Banglish, Roman Urdu, Greeklish, Roman Persian and fifteen more. Type in Latin letters, get the native script back.

Hinglish is still what TypeAura is really built for — type the way you actually speak and it converts to clean English, or the reverse.

Does it work offline?

Typing, glide typing, suggestions, themes, emoji and clipboard history all work fully offline. The AI features — translation, the Lens, AI Chat and the tools — need an internet connection, because the model runs on a server rather than on your phone.

What happens when I run out of daily tokens?

The keyboard keeps working normally — only the AI actions pause. Your counter resets automatically at midnight. If you hit the limit regularly, Pro gives you 5× the budget and Max gives you 15×.

Is the keyboard layout right for my language?

There are seven layouts — QWERTY, AZERTY, QWERTZ, and dedicated variants for Spanish (Ñ), German (Ü Ö Ä), Danish/Norwegian (Å Æ Ø) and Swedish/Finnish (Å Ö Ä). Those letters get real keys, not long-presses, because they are letters of the alphabet rather than accents.

Your country picks the starting layout, but it is only a starting point — it is a setting, so switch it any time. Accented letters beyond the layout are a long-press away, and the currency key types the sign your country actually spends in.

Which Android versions work? Is there an iPhone version?

TypeAura runs on Android 5.0 and above. There is no iOS version — iOS does not allow the kind of system-wide overlay the Floating Lens depends on. There is a Windows desktop app with the same AI tools and a global-hotkey Lens.

I'm Himanshu Kashyap, a developer who's been frustrated with keyboards that don't understand how Indians actually type. We all mix Hindi and English constantly, but every keyboard out there treats it like a problem instead of a superpower.

So I built TypeAura — a keyboard that doesn't judge your Hinglish, but makes it shine. With AI translation built right into the keyboard, you can type the way you think and sound the way you want.
